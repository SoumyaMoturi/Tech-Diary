**Analysis of Algorithm:**
Analysis of algorithms is the determination of the amount of time and space resources required to execute it.

**Importance of Analysis of Algorithm:**

 * we can predict the behavior of an algorithm without implementing it on a 
   specific computer.
 * It is much more convenient to have simple measures for the efficiency of an 
   algorithm than to implement the algorithm and test the efficiency every 
   time.
 * Though,it is impossible to predict the exact behaviour of an algorithm, 
   analysis is thus only an approximation.By analyzing different algorithms, 
   we can compare them to determine the best one for our purpose.

**Types of Algorithm Analysis:**

  * Best Case
  * Worst Case
  * Average Case
